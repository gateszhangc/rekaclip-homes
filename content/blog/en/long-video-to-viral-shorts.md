Most creators do not lack footage—they lack **selection**. Viral shorts are rarely random; they are compressed stories with a clear payoff in the first two seconds.

## Step 1: Hunt for change, not length

Look for moments where something shifts:

- a reaction spike,
- a punchline landing,
- a mistake recovered,
- a number revealed on screen.

Reka Clip scores these automatically, but your eye still wins on context. Favorite three candidates before you export anything.

## Step 2: Open with the payoff

Vertical feeds punish slow intros. Structure clips as:

1. **Hook frame** — the funniest or most surprising frame first.
2. **Context** — one sentence of setup, on-screen text if needed.
3. **Payoff** — let the moment breathe; do not cut early for “pacing.”

## Step 3: Caption for mute and sound-on

Write captions as **subtitles**, not transcripts. Short lines, emphasis on verbs, and a readable safe zone. Reka Clip’s caption styles are tuned for thumb-stopping contrast—turn them on unless the visual itself is the hook.

## Step 4: Ship variants, not perfection

One VOD can yield:

- a 12s punchline clip,
- a 28s story clip,
- a 45s “you won’t believe what happened” clip.

Publish two variants 48 hours apart and let retention data pick the winner.

## Step 5: Close the loop with Boost

Clips that sit on disk do not trend. When a short clears 70% average view duration on one platform, Boost it to the others with platform-specific titles—not copy-paste descriptions.
