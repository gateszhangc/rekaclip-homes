Reka Clip accepts YouTube and Twitch URLs today, with more platforms on the roadmap. Each source has different **density** of highlight-worthy moments.

## YouTube (VODs and long uploads)

**Strengths:** stable audio, chapter markers, predictable pacing.

**Tips:**

- Prefer videos with clear speech segments; music-only montages score poorly.
- Use Moments for essay-style videos; Compilation for gameplay montages.
- Thumbnail frames from YouTube often differ from your vertical crop—re-check the first frame.

## Twitch (streams and VODs)

**Strengths:** reactive chat energy, frequent surprise beats.

**Tips:**

- Clip chat reactions only when the visual carries the joke—chat alone does not export well.
- Stream VODs longer than three hours benefit from chaptering mentally: treat each game or guest segment as its own Clip job.
- Audio spikes from alerts are common; trim 0.5s before/after alert sounds when possible.

## Rights and etiquette

Only clip content you own or are licensed to repurpose. For collaborations, credit guests in Boost metadata and descriptions.

## Upload when links fail

If a VOD is sub-only or geo-blocked, download locally and upload the MP4. The same Clip pipeline applies—only the ingest path changes.

Platform quirks change; the workflow does not: **link or upload → select template → caption → Boost**.
