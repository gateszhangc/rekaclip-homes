Captions are not accessibility extras anymore—they are **creative**. On vertical video, they often carry more narrative weight than the audio.

## Design for the thumb zone

Keep text inside the center 80% of the frame. Reka Clip’s safe defaults avoid the right-side engagement stack on most apps. If you add stickers or B-roll, re-check overlap before export.

## Rhythm beats completeness

AI transcripts are a starting point. Edit for:

- **line breaks** at natural pauses,
- **emphasis** on one word per sentence,
- **deletion** of filler (“um”, “like”, false starts).

A 22-second clip with eight caption lines beats a wall of text.

## Match tone to brand

- Gaming: punchy, ALL CAPS on reactions sparingly.
- Education: calmer pacing, numbers spelled consistently.
- Podcast: speaker labels only when two voices overlap.

## Test mute-first

Watch once with sound off. If the story is unclear, rewrite captions before you tweak color grades.

## Export checklist

- Contrast passes on OLED and LCD previews
- No captions touching face landmarks
- Hook line visible in frame zero

Small caption edits compound—teams that batch-caption in Clip routinely ship 2× more shorts per VOD.
