Reka Clip is an AI video clipping workspace built for creators who live on long-form content but grow on short-form platforms.

Paste a YouTube or Twitch link, or upload a file, and the system finds high-retention moments, reframes for vertical, and adds captions tuned for silent scroll. You stay in control of what ships—Boost handles the rest when you are ready to publish.

## The problem it solves

Manual clipping burns time:

- scrubbing VODs for peaks,
- exporting multiple aspect ratios,
- rewriting hooks for TikTok and Reels,
- scheduling the same clip across channels.

Reka Clip compresses that loop into one surface: **find → refine → publish**.

## What you get out of the box

1. **Clip** — moment detection, templates (Moments vs Compilation), aspect ratio, split layout, captions, and export resolution presets.
2. **Boost** — tiered publishing workflows, tags, and platform-ready packaging so clips do not die in a downloads folder.
3. **Feature** — tracking and analytics hooks so you can see what actually moved the needle.

## Who it is for

- Streamers turning VODs into Shorts
- Podcasters shipping audiograms and highlight reels
- Marketing teams repurposing webinars
- Editors who want a first pass before fine cuts

## A sane first session

Start small: one 45–90 minute VOD, Moments template, 9:16, captions on. Review the top five suggestions, tweak two titles, and publish one clip through Boost. That single loop teaches you more than tweaking every knob on day one.
